The army is divided into two major branches:

- **Space Corps**: fleet and orbital operations.
- **Telluric Corps**: ground forces and exosoldiers.

It is jointly commanded by the **Council of Guardians** and the **Warlords**.

### The Warlords

The **Warlords** are high-ranking officers, each controlling an armada (the largest army detachment). They also embody a senior civil servant role, close to ministers in their political responsibilities.

**Official selection:** Warlords are officially chosen through a **combat-tournament** against the most recently appointed Warlord. This tournament combines:
- A 1v1 duel in an arcanotechnological simulated environment
- A strategic RTS-style confrontation

**Political reality:** In practice, it is often a political choice made by the Council of Guardians, and the outcome of the combat is predetermined.

**Number and power:** The Empire typically has around **twenty Warlords**. They are subordinates of the Council, but they wield considerable power and influence. Some, though rarely, can be very ambitious and seek to challenge the Council.

**Attire:** Warlords wear ceremonial uniforms during public appearances. Outside of these, they dress normally. In combat, they use high-technology exoskeletons or battle armor.

The Empire's strength relies less on the average quality of its soldiers than on **the variety and specialization of its elite units**, serving these experienced strategists through direct and efficient chains of command. In wartime, the coordinated use of these specialized groups allows them to overwhelm the enemy through a multiplicity of different tactical approaches.
