### The Opals

The **Opals** constitute one of the Empire's most renowned elite units. Capable of single-handedly influencing the course of a battle, they embody imperial power and tactical flexibility.

**Adaptive equipment:** The Opals have **three types of exoskeletons** depending on their mission:

1. **Public presence**: Lightweight exoskeleton, face visible, white cape
2. **Combat missions**: Heavy exoskeleton, face protected by visor
3. **Special missions**: Stealth exoskeleton, with adaptive camouflage

**Visual identity:** Their exoskeleton is white with gold and blue accents, allowing immediate identification on the battlefield or during public appearances.

**General principle:** Most elite units wear high-technology exoskeletons or battle armor adapted to their specific missions. A Hero or experienced soldier is able to recognize them more or less well depending on their experience.
