---
title: "Secret Units"
order: 5
---

**Secret units** represent the most classified echelon of the imperial army. Unlike the known intelligence agencies of other civilizations, **their very existence is secret** from the general population.

**Methods and objectives:**
- All methods are authorized, including psychological warfare
- Information manipulation and dissemination of false news
- Infiltration and dissolution of rebel groups
- Provocation of internal rebellions within enemy organizations

**Relations with Heroes:** Although Heroes are distinct autonomous units, secret units may recruit Heroes for specific missions requiring their exceptional skills.

**Classification:** Their methods and reports are entirely classified. Missions are communicated orally only, with no written records.
