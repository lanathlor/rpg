| Rank               | Type          | Description                                                                |
| ------------------ | ------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Infantry**       | Standard      | Basic units, lightly trained but numerous.                                 |
| **Specialists**    | Operational   | Units capable of autonomous and specific missions.                         |
| **Elite**          | Superior      | Capable of single-handedly influencing the course of a battle (e.g., the _Opals_). |
| **Secret**         | Unknown       | Classified methods and reports, oral-only missions.                        |
| **Heroes (Rank H)** | Autonomous  | Legendary units, ranked from H-6 (recruit) to H-1 (icon).                 |

**Heroes** are exceptional soldiers, free in their methods. They choose their missions but must maintain their own equipment and training. Their semi-independent status makes them both admired and feared.

**Identification:** Most Heroes get **discreet tattoos** to show their rank among themselves, but nothing visible in public. In authorized combat zones, they wear their combat gear. In civilian urban areas, they wear normal clothing.

**Housing:** Heroes have official quarters on the ecumenopolis Prime and at army headquarters, where Heroes are regularly stationed.
