In a society where the majority of citizens have considerable free time, entertainment is **crucial** for maintaining social cohesion and general contentment.

### Arcanotechnological Sports

**Obsolete mechanical sports:** Running and other purely physical sports have become **niche sports** with arcanotechnological advancement, although they retain purist enthusiasts.

**Spectacular sports using Arcanotechnology:**

-   **Arcanotechnological vehicle racing**: High-speed competitions using arcanotechnological propulsion
-   **Exoskeleton arena combat**: Tactical and spectacular confrontations
-   **Partial anti-gravity sports**: Allow physical feats impossible under normal gravity while still leaving room for athletic skill

### E-sports and Strategy Games

**Arcanotechnological e-sports** are heavily promoted:

-   **Augmented reality strategy games**: Combining tactics and coordination
-   **Combat simulations**: Indirectly preparing for war while entertaining
-   **Professional competitions**: Attracting sponsorships and massive audiences

### Participatory Arts

The arts are **very participatory** in imperial society, far more than in pre-post-scarcity civilizations.

**Relaxed copyright laws:** Intellectual property laws are very permissive, encouraging popular creativity and cultural remixing. Many ordinary people create **art, music, theater**, etc.

**Mass creative culture:** This participatory approach creates a vibrant and dynamic culture where the distinction between professional and amateur creators is blurred.

### Mass Culture and Celebrities

Imperial society has **less celebrity culture** than historical civilizations. With so much free time, people often prefer to **participate** rather than simply **consume** passively.

**Holodramas and narrative media:** Although they exist and are popular, they coexist with a strong amateur creation culture.
