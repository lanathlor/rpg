### Family Structure

**Family is more open to friends** in imperial society. People have more time to create social bonds outside the biological family, and the life expectancy of 150-200 years allows for deep and lasting relationships across multiple generations.

**Marriage:** Still exists but is **less formal** than historically. Unions and separations are fluid and socially accepted.

**Children:** Encouraged to **follow their passions** rather than traditional roles or economic imperatives.

### Sexuality and Gender

Imperial society is **very open** regarding sexuality and gender. It values diversity and individual expression.

**Fluid gender norms:** Gender concepts are flexible and personal. Sexuality is seen as a natural part of life.

**Social non-issue:** These questions are no longer even subjects of debate in this society. Complete equality is so deeply integrated that it is no longer noticed.

### Social Taboos

Taboos are **few** in imperial society. Primarily:

-   **Illegal activities**: Pirates, arms traffickers, forbidden technologies
-   **Moderate anti-imperialism accepted**: Being critical of the Council is tolerated as long as it is not militant
-   **"Rebel" in fashion vs real rebel**: Being a "rebel" is accepted and even valued. Being a "real" rebel threatening the established order is not.

### Dress Codes

Imperial society favors **vestimentary egalitarianism**. Most people dress functionally and comfortably.

**Uniformed professions:** Certain professions wear uniforms, but only at work. Outside of work, normal clothing.

**High-end arcanotechnological fashion:** For the elite, haute couture clothing exists using Arcanotechnology for **impressive aesthetic effects**:

-   Luminous effects
-   Color change
-   Texture change
-   Floating
-   Adaptive fabrics that change according to the environment or mood

**Rebel fashion:** An equivalent to the "punk" movement exists, characterized by **tattoos and symbols** of dissent, but channeled toward aesthetics rather than real subversion.

**Regional styles:** Clothing varies across planetary systems, but **imperial identity dominates**. Some retro styles are in fashion, more or less depending on the planet.

### Inter-System Social Interactions

There is **local pride**, but **imperial identity is predominant**. People travel extensively between systems, and inter-system marriages are common.

Inhabitants of **Federated Systems** (formerly integrated factions) and **Imperial Provinces** (founded by the Empire) interact without significant discrimination, although cultural nuances persist.
