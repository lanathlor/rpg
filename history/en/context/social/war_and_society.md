### Perception of the Acéras War

After more than 200 years of conflict, most people are **weary** of it but understand the necessity of defending themselves. The war is seen as a **necessary evil**.

**Glorification of veterans:** The Empire glorifies its veterans and creates **war heroes** to maintain morale. However, a **growing pacifist movement** criticizes the perpetuation of the conflict.

**Traumatized generation:** With a life expectancy of 150-200 years, some citizens have lived through **the entire Acéras war** since its beginning, creating a generation deeply scarred by two centuries of conflict.

### Mobilization and Social Impact

During the final phase (5755), the **mobilization of 3-5% of the population** affects all families. Almost everyone knows someone serving on the front.

This massive mobilization in a post-scarcity society accustomed to comfort creates tensions, but also a sense of common purpose that temporarily unifies the Empire.
