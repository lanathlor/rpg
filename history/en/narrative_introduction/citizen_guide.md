*Official Welcome Document - Edition 5755*

Welcome to the Human Empire, the greatest achievement of our species! Whether you were born a citizen or recently integrated from a federated system, this guide will help you navigate our magnificent civilization.

## Your New Life: Freedom and Prosperity

**Congratulations!** As an imperial citizen, you benefit from:

✓ **Free housing** - Every citizen receives housing suited to their needs
✓ **Guaranteed food and healthcare** - Never again hunger or untreated illness
✓ **Life expectancy of 150-200 years** - Thanks to our advanced arcanotechnological medicine
✓ **Optional work** - Choose to contribute based on your passions, not your needs!
✓ **Universal education** - Unlimited access to human knowledge
✓ **Democratic representation** - Your voice matters at the Imperial Assembly!

## The Council of Guardians: Eternal Wisdom

Our five immortal Guardians have guided humanity for 5,755 years with incomparable wisdom:

- **Sérac** - Our brilliant strategist, leading the defense against the Acéras threat
- **Théon** - Architect of our arcanotechnological miracles
- **Veyra** - Opening new worlds for humanity
- **Olis** - Guaranteeing health and longevity for all
- **Carène** - Orchestrating our economy of abundance

Their long-term vision - impossible for us mortals - ensures the continued stability and prosperity of the Empire.

## Arcanotechnology: Science of the Flux

Forget the superstitions of old! Arcanotechnology is an **exact science** that converts Primic energy into usable forces. Your neural implants, the warpgates enabling instantaneous travel (for priority missions), and even your adaptive clothing - everything uses this revolutionary technology.

**Fun fact:** Did you know that our arcanotechnological industrial production has a near-zero energy cost? That's why we can afford post-scarcity!

## Unlimited Opportunities

**Work is optional, but rewarding!** Choose your contribution:

- **Sciences and research** - Push the boundaries of knowledge
- **Military service** - Defend humanity (exceptional social benefits!)
- **Arts and culture** - Express yourself freely (copyright is virtually nonexistent!)
- **Exploration** - Discover new worlds
- **Or simply... enjoy!** - Leisure is a valid and respected choice

*Note: Physically demanding and dangerous work is particularly respected and well-compensated.*

## Prime and Nexus: Jewels of the Empire

Our two ecumenopoleis, each housing nearly **2 trillion citizens**, represent the pinnacle of human organization:

- **Prime**: Seat of the Council and the Assembly, political heart of the Empire
- **Nexus**: Secondary administrative center, galactic commercial hub

With their suspended parks, their infinite vertical levels, and their dazzling architecture, they embody our collective grandeur. Space is precious there, but the experience is unique!

## The Acéras War: A Temporary Challenge

Yes, we have been at war for two centuries. But under the wise leadership of Sérac and the Council, victory is near! Only 3-5% of our population serves on the front - a modest effort to protect our way of life.

The Acéras, xenophobic and aggressive, have rejected all our peace offers. But don't worry: our legendary Heroes, our elite Opals, and our superior strategies guarantee our final triumph.

## Freedom of Expression

The Empire values your voice! Criticize, question, express yourself - it is your right. We know that a citizen who can voice their frustrations is a happy citizen.

*Of course, violent action against the established order remains illegal. But why would you want to destroy paradise?*

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I travel freely?**
A: Absolutely! FTL travel takes 5-10 days depending on your ship. Instantaneous warpgates are reserved for priorities, but who's in a rush with a 200-year lifespan?

**Q: What if I don't want to work at all?**
A: No problem! Enjoy the arts, anti-grav sports, arcanotechnological simulations. Life is meant to be lived.

**Q: Are all drugs really legal?**
A: Yes! We treat excessive consumption with compassion, not criminalization. Your body, your choice.

**Q: Can I start a business?**
A: Of course! If it becomes strategically important, the Empire will generously purchase it, making you wealthy. Win-win!

**Q: How can I serve the Empire?**
A: Vote for your representatives at the Assembly, contribute according to your talents, or simply live happily - a fulfilled citizen strengthens the Empire!
