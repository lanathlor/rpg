This section presents the historical and political context of the game's universe.
It serves as a reference for players and the GM to situate events, factions, and the role of Arcanotechnology in the world.

---

## Key Periods

- **Year 0**: **The Foundation** - The Council of Guardians unifies the first human factions, creating the Empire.
- ~3500 years after the foundation: the Empire begins its large-scale expansion.
- 5527 years after the foundation: first contact with the Acéras, beginning of the war.
- 5764 years after the foundation: peace is declared.
- 5863 years after the foundation: the Empire federates all human clans under its banner.
