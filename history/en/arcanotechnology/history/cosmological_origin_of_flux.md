A recent discovery has revolutionized our understanding of the nature of the Flux: **it does not exist in a single universe, but in a multiverse**.

### Primordial Formation

The Flux is created during the **birth of a universe**:

-   At the moment of the Big Bang, the rapid expansion of spacetime generates the primic field as a byproduct of universal creation
-   The Flux is born in a state of extremely low entropy, "frozen" in a metastable configuration by the initial conditions of the universe
-   It is an intrinsic characteristic of the formation of a spacetime continuum

### Continuous Generation

The Flux is not solely primordial - it continues to be **generated during the expansion of the universe**:

-   Like space itself, the primic field grows with cosmic expansion
-   New regions of space bring with them Flux in its metastable state
-   This process is extremely slow but constant

### The Fate of Ancient Universes

In a multiverse, universes have different ages:

-   **Young universes** are rich in Flux - expansion generates new field faster than it is converted
-   **Mature universes** (like ours) are in equilibrium - generation and consumption approximately compensate each other
-   **Very ancient universes** may exhibit Flux depletion - expansion has slowed or ceased, consumption exceeds generation

### Practical Implications

This cosmological understanding explains:

-   Why certain galactic regions exhibit different Flux densities (local spacetime dynamics)
-   Why the Flux does not seem to deplete at the observable scale (continuous cosmic generation)
-   Why the Guardians carefully monitor consumption rates (fragile equilibrium)

The Council of Guardians maintains a **galactic monitoring network** to measure Flux flows (no pun intended) and ensure that civilizational consumption does not exceed local cosmic regeneration.

### Civilizational Responsibility

Although the universe continues to generate Flux, Imperial civilization could theoretically:

-   Consume Flux locally faster than it regenerates
-   Create "dead zones" depleted of Flux
-   Compromise access to this resource for future generations

The Council of Guardians therefore maintains strict control over Flux exploitation, aware that this extraordinary resource also carries immense responsibility.
