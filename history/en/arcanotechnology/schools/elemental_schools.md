### Fire School

One of the first schools mastered, **Fire** converts the Flux directly into **thermal energy** by increasing molecular agitation. The arcanotechnic process specifically targets the vibrational modes of molecular bonds.

**Physical mechanism:** Flux -> molecular kinetic energy (heat)

**Applications:** Controlled combustion, thermal propulsion, arcanotechnic forging

### Frost School

Discovered in opposition to fire, the **Frost** school uses a deeply **endothermic** Flux conversion - meaning the conversion itself requires the absorption of heat from the environment to complete.

The mechanism relies on a special electronic configuration (configuration delta) that triggers a Flux decay toward a **kinetic dampening field state**. This conversion is endothermic and can only progress by extracting thermal energy from surrounding molecules. The Flux transforms into a field that:

1. **Absorbs the kinetic energy** of molecules to complete its own conversion
2. **Slows molecular vibrations** by transferring their energy to the conversion process
3. **Then dissipates** into non-thermal field states (potentially into the quantum vacuum)

Unlike Fire which releases thermal energy, Frost **consumes** thermal energy as fuel for its conversion. The net effect is rapid and controlled cooling. It is like an endothermic chemical reaction at the quantum field scale.

**Key points:**

-   The Flux is never reconstituted - it continues its irreversible decay
-   The absorbed thermal energy fuels the conversion, it is not stored
-   The final effect dissipates into the quantum vacuum or other non-recoverable states
-   The more Flux converted, the more intense the cooling

**Physical mechanism:** Flux + molecular kinetic energy -> dissipative field state -> net cooling

**Applications:** Cryogenization, molecular deceleration, crystallization, preservation

### Electricity School

**Electricity** converts the Flux into **electrical potential difference** by separating charges at the quantum level. The arcanotechnic process directly generates electronic currents without going through an intermediate thermal conversion.

**Physical mechanism:** Flux -> charge separation -> electric current

**Applications:** Energy production, communication systems, paralyzing weapons

### Light School

**Light** converts the Flux directly into **photons** via a controlled annihilation process. The frequency and coherence of generated photons can be finely tuned by the arcanotechnician. A major discovery was the ability to **solidify light** into tangible defensive structures.

**Physical mechanism:** Flux -> photons (electromagnetic radiation) -> solidified light

**Applications:** Holography, optical camouflage, laser communications, tactical blinding, solid light shields

### Shadow School

Counterpoint to light, **Shadow** reconverts incident photons into dissipated Flux, creating a **total absorption** of visible light. This arcanotechnic process generates a zone of darkness that disrupts perception and optical sensors.

**Physical mechanism:** Photons -> Flux (dissipated) -> absence of light

**Applications:** Camouflage, infiltration, area concealment, sensory disruption, invisibility to sensors
