### Aerokinetic School

**Aerokinetics** controls atmospheric flows and air pressure. Developed for terraforming, it is now essential for environmental control.

**Applications:** Weather control, wind propulsion, breathing in hostile environments

### Gravitonic School

**Gravitonics** is the most complex of the schools - it converts the Flux into **local spacetime curvature**. This arcanotechnic process requires extraordinarily high conversion densities and quantum precision. Basic applications (weight alteration, levitation) are accessible to trained practitioners, but advanced manipulations (spatial deformation, gravitational lensing) remain the exclusive domain of the Guardians.

**Physical mechanism:** Flux -> spacetime curvature -> gravitational effect

**Applications:** Weight alteration, controlled levitation, projectile deflection, antigravity propulsion, spatial deformation (Guardian level)

### Hydrodynamic School

**Hydrodynamics** controls fluids, particularly water. This school was crucial for the colonization of aquatic worlds.

**Applications:** Water purification, hydraulic propulsion, tide control

### Kinesthetic School

**Kinesthetics** converts the Flux into **pure kinetic energy** applied to macroscopic objects. Kinesthetic arcanotechnology allows imparting momentum without going through intermediate forces.

**Physical mechanism:** Flux -> momentum (mv) -> acceleration

**Applications:** Strength augmentation, impact absorption, kinetic projectiles
