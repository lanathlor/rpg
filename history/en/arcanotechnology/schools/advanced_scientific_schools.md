### Ballistic School

**Ballistics** optimizes trajectories and projectile velocity. Born from military necessities, it dominates modern arcanotechnic armament.

**Applications:** Missile guidance, ballistic optimization, railgun

### Magnetic School

**Magnetism** controls magnetic fields to manipulate metals and charged particles. This school is fundamental in the space industry.

**Applications:** Magnetic levitation, propulsion, electromagnetic shielding

### Sonic School

**Sonics** manipulates sound waves and vibrations. Beyond mere noise, it allows resonating with matter itself.

**Applications:** Long-distance communication, destructive resonance, arcanotechnic sonar

### Chronodynamic School

**Chronodynamics** is the most mysterious school, claiming to manipulate local temporal flow. Its very existence is debated in academic circles.

**Applications:** Perceptual deceleration, local acceleration, temporary stasis

### Quantum School

**Quantum** exploits the quantum properties of matter to create probabilistic effects.

**Applications:** Quantum teleportation, entanglement, state superposition
