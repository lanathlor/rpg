### Bioarcanotechnic School

**Bioarcanotechnology** interfaces the Flux with biological and cybernetic systems. This school allows the manipulation of nerve impulses, amplification of cognitive capabilities, and the creation of synergies between living organisms and technological implants. Applications range from perceptual enhancement to the optimization of military exoskeletons.

**Applications:** Nerve impulse manipulation, cognitive and perceptual enhancement, neural-cybernetic interface, exoskeleton amplification, mind-to-mind information sharing

### Biometabolic School

**Biometabolic** manipulates metabolic processes and vital energy at the cellular level. This school allows both the extraction of vital energy from living organisms (offensive applications) and theoretically the stimulation of biological processes (medical applications, though less common on the battlefield).

**Applications:** Vital drain, metabolic energy extraction, cellular process disruption, metabolic stimulation (medical)
