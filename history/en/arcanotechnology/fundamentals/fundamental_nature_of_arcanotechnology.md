---
title: "Fundamental Nature of Arcanotechnology"
order: 1
---

Arcanotechnology constitutes a **fundamental potential energy**, called **primic energy**, omnipresent in the very structure of the universe. Intrinsic to all matter, it permeates every atom, every particle, forming the energetic substrate of physical reality.

Practitioners - called **channelers** - have learned to extract and convert this primic energy into different forms of measurable energy: thermal energy (heat), electromagnetic (light, electricity), mechanical (gravity, pressure), or even biological and informational.

Each **School of arcanotechnology** corresponds to a **specific conversion** of arcanotechnic energy toward a particular type of physical energy:

- The **Fire** school allows converting the Flux into molecular kinetic energy (heat)
- The **Electricity** school allows transforming it into an ordered electromagnetic field
- The **Light** school allows generating a coherent and confined photonic flux
- The **Kinesthetic** school allows producing mechanical work

This scientific understanding of arcanotechnology has revolutionized the Empire's technological approaches.
