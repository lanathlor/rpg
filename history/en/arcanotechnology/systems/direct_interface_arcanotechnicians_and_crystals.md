An important development was the discovery that **trained arcanotechnicians can directly interface with crystals** without requiring the control modulator:

-   A practitioner can generate the necessary electronic patterns themselves to trigger conversion of the Flux concentrated in the crystal
-   This eliminates the need for the heavy arcanotechnic modulator (saving ~40-50 kg)
-   The crystal acts simply as a **Flux reservoir** that the arcanotechnician can exploit
-   Versatility remains that of the practitioner - the crystal just provides more "fuel"

### Tactical Advantages

-   **Extended endurance**: An arcanotechnician with a crystal can operate much longer before exhaustion
-   **Amplified power**: Access to Flux density superior to ambient Flux
-   **Portability**: A crystal alone weighs only a few kilograms, easily transportable
-   **Flexibility**: The practitioner retains their ability to adapt conversions according to needs

### Limitations

-   Requires a trained arcanotechnician (does not democratize access)
-   The crystal remains subject to the same recharging and explosion problems
-   The practitioner must consciously manage the discharge rate to avoid killing the crystal
-   Direct interface is more mentally fatiguing than using ambient Flux

This crystal-practitioner synergy has become standard in elite military units and for civilian arcanotechnicians operating in low Flux density zones.
