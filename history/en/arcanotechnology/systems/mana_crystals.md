One of the greatest arcanotechnic breakthroughs was the creation of **Flux crystals** - specially designed crystalline matrices that exploit the electron-Flux coupling mechanism. These crystals act as **Flux reservoirs** with high negative entropy and as controllable converters.

### Operating Principle

The crystalline structure is ingeniously designed to combine two functions:

1. **Flux Concentration**: The geometry of the crystal lattice creates a potential well that attracts and concentrates ambient Flux. The atoms of the crystal interact weakly with the field, creating a region of locally elevated Flux density.

2. **Crystal Lattice Electrons**: The conduction electrons in the crystal can be stimulated (by electric current, mechanical vibrations, or even heat) to adopt the configurations necessary for coupling with the Flux. Unlike neural systems, crystalline electrons are much more predictable and controllable.

3. **Controlled Conversion**: When an appropriate stimulus is applied to the crystal, the lattice electrons trigger the conversion of concentrated Flux. The type of stimulus and the crystalline structure determine the decay channel (heat, light, electricity, etc.).

4. **Recharging**: Once the Flux is extracted and converted, the crystal must be "recharged" by re-exposing it to a region of dense ambient Flux. The concentration process takes time but is passive.

### Technological Advantages

-   **Accessibility**: No neural training required
-   **Reliability**: Reproducible and predictable conversion
-   **Portability**: Can be integrated into compact devices
-   **Safety**: No risk of mental overload for the user

These crystals have democratized access to arcanotechnology, allowing even non-practitioners to use devices powered by Flux conversion. From simple light sources to energy weapons, Flux crystals power modern civilization.
