---
title: "The Future of Arcanotechnology"
order: 10
---

The frontiers of arcanotechnology continue to expand. Researchers are exploring:

-   New hybrid conversion modes
-   Improvement of conversion efficiency (currently < 10%)
-   Cybernetic neural interfaces for Flux manipulation
-   Arcanotechnic applications at the quantum scale
-   Galactic Flux mapping
-   Modulator miniaturization (target: 20-30 kg reduction)
-   Synthetic crystals with multiple affinities
-   Secure recharging protocols to reduce explosion risk
