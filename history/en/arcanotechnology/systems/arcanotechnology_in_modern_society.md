order: 1

Today, arcanotechnology permeates every aspect of Imperial society. From simple mana crystals that power household devices to arcanotechnic weapons that equip soldiers, the Flux is omnipresent.

### The Arcanotechnic Academies

Every major world in the Empire has its academy where individuals exhibiting a Flux affinity are trained. The selection process is rigorous, as a poorly trained arcanotechnician represents a danger to themselves and those around them.

### The Order of Arcanotechnicians

The **Order of Arcanotechnicians** regulates the practice of the Flux throughout the Empire. Under the direct supervision of the Council of Guardians, the Order:

-   Certifies qualified practitioners
-   Monitors Flux usage
-   Develops new applications
-   Investigates illicit uses
