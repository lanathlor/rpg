The military sector depends entirely on automated systems:

-   **Armament**: Plasma cannons, arcanotechnic missile launchers, point defense systems
-   **Energy shields**: Continuous Flux conversion into defensive barriers
-   **Space propulsion**: Gravitonic and kinesthetic engines for vessels
-   **Countermeasure systems**: Arcanotechnic jamming, energy projectile absorption
-   **Combat drones**: Autonomous platforms equipped with multi-channel converters
