These limitations explain the current doctrine of deployment:

### Preferred Uses of Crystal Systems

-   **Spaceships**: Weight is not a problem, conversions are predictable (propulsion, shields), crystals can be confined in armored zones
-   **Heavy ground artillery**: Fixed installations where mass is acceptable, limited and controlled usage
-   **Defense installations**: Planetary shields, stationary anti-aircraft systems
-   **Civilian infrastructure**: Energy plants, large-scale climate regulation

### Preference for Human Arcanotechnicians

-   **Ground combat**: Mobility, versatility, no risk of explosion
-   **Special operations**: Rapid adaptation, multiple capabilities, stealth
-   **Complex situations**: Human judgment, improvisation, tactical creativity
-   **Extended missions**: Humans "recharge" naturally without mortal risk

A military adage sums up the situation well: _"A crystal can give you more power. An arcanotechnician gives you more options. In combat, options win."_

### Systemic Vulnerabilities

Beyond physical limitations:

-   **Complexity**: A failure can paralyze entire sectors of the economy
-   **Vulnerability to countermeasures**: Jammers can disrupt conversions
-   **Initial cost**: Installing arcanotechnic infrastructure is extremely expensive
-   **Dependence on ambient Flux**: Regions with low Flux density are problematic

This complex dependence means that a major disruption in Flux supply or sabotage of arcanotechnic infrastructure could have catastrophic consequences for the Empire.
