---
title: "Industrial Applications"
order: 5
---

Automated systems have transformed Imperial industry:

-   **Manufacturing**: Arcanotechnic forges that generate exact temperatures, plasma cutting, directed energy welding
-   **Energy production**: Large-scale conversion plants powering entire cities
-   **Materials processing**: Instantaneous cryogenization, metallic quenching, controlled crystallization
-   **Construction**: Gravitonic levitation systems for lifting massive structures
-   **Agriculture**: Climate regulation, bio-arcanotechnic growth stimulation
