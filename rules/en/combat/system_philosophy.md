title: "System Philosophy"
order: 11
---

> Combat is built on two principles: **simplicity of rolls** and **clarity of values**.
> The player rolls only one die to attack; the target rolls nothing to defend.
> The fixed values for Defense and Resistances make the system fluid yet tactical.

The system's priorities:

- **Physical unification**: a single physical reality, no "magical vs physical" distinction.
- **Resistances based on attack nature**: mechanical, radiative, or internal.
- **Narrative coherence**: the Flux creates real physical phenomena that follow the laws of physics.
- **Clear and measurable defenses**: RMEC, RRAD, RINT.
- **Natural progression**: through equipment, statistics, and Flux mastery.
- **Reading fluidity**: for the GM and the players.

**Fundamental principle:** It is not the source of the attack that determines the applicable resistance, but the physical nature of the effect produced. An arcanotechnological fireball deals radiative damage (RRAD), just like a conventional flame.
