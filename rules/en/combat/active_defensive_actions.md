---
title: "Active Defensive Actions"
order: 6
tags: [Defensive action, Parry, Dodge, Block]
---

A character may forgo attacking to actively defend themselves.
These actions provide temporary bonuses to the Defense Score or Resistances.

| Action             | Effect                                                                                                            | Duration            |
| ------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| **Parry**          | Make an **opposed STR or DEX roll** against the physical attack. On success, the attack is blocked.               | Instant             |
| **Dodge**          | Roll **d20 + DEX** against the opponent's attack roll. On success, the attack misses.                             | Instant             |
| **Take Cover**     | +2 to ranged Defense and +1 to RMEC/RRAD.                                                                        | Until next turn     |
| **Hold the Line**  | If adjacent to an ally, +1 to your Defense and to that ally's Defense.                                            | Until end of turn   |
