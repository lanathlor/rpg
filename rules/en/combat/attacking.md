An attack proceeds in two steps: **attack roll**, then **damage roll**.

### 1. Attack Roll

> **d20 + Attack Stat + weapon bonus >= target's Defense Score**

| Weapon or Spell Type          | Attack Stat Used     |
| ----------------------------- | -------------------- |
| Heavy weapon / 2-handed       | Strength (STR)       |
| Light weapon / 1-handed       | Dexterity (DEX)      |
| Ranged weapon                 | Precision (PRE)      |
| Offensive spell               | Intelligence (INT)   |

-   **Natural 20** -> critical success (unstoppable attack, damage doubled).
-   **Natural 1** -> critical failure (weapon jams, spell fails).

### 2. Damage Roll

-   Roll the dice indicated on the weapon or spell.
-   Determine the physical nature of the attack (mechanical, radiative, or internal).
-   Subtract the corresponding resistance (RMEC, RRAD, or RINT).
-   Apply the result to the target's hit points.
-   If the target is reduced to 0 HP -> they are knocked out of combat.

**Correspondence Examples:**

-   Sword, bullet, projectile -> **RMEC**
-   Fire, laser, lightning -> **RRAD**
-   Life drain, necrosis, corruption -> **RINT**
