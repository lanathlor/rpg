Maneuvers encompass all non-offensive actions: item use, environment interaction, special interactions, etc.

> Associated roll: **d20 + Appropriate Stat (often DEX, INT, or PER)**.
> The GM sets the difficulty based on the context (see "Dice Roll" section).

Characters with the **Strategist** skill can perform **two maneuvers per turn**.

### Maneuver Examples by Statistic

#### **Charisma-based Maneuvers (CHA)**
- **Intimidation**: Demoralize an enemy (CHA roll) -> -2 to the target's attacks for 2 turns
- **Rally**: Encourage allies (CHA roll) -> +1 to an ally's next roll within a 10m radius
- **Quick Negotiation**: Convince an enemy to temporarily switch sides (CHA roll, very hard)
- **Tactical Command**: Coordinate movements (CHA roll) -> an ally can move for free

#### **Perception-based Maneuvers (PER)**
- **Tactical Analysis**: Spot enemy weaknesses (PER roll) -> reveals the target's defense bonuses
- **Ambush Detection**: Anticipate a surprise attack (PER roll) -> +2 to the group's initiative
- **Battlefield Surveillance**: Locate threats (PER roll) -> alerts to hidden enemies
- **Reading Intentions**: Predict the enemy's next action (PER roll) -> defense bonus against that attack

#### **Intelligence-based Maneuvers (INT)**
- **Technical Analysis**: Identify equipment weak points (INT roll) -> -2 to the target's armor bonuses
- **Ballistic Calculation**: Optimize group fire (INT roll) -> +2 to allied ranged attacks this turn
- **Quick Hacking**: Temporarily disable an enemy system (INT roll) -> penalty to exoskeletons
- **Adaptive Strategy**: Reorganize the formation (INT roll) -> +1 defense to all allies

#### **Constitution-based Maneuvers (CON)**
- **Second Wind**: Recover stamina (CON roll) -> regain 2d4 HP (once per combat)
- **Heroic Resistance**: Ignore pain (CON roll) -> immunity to injury penalties for 3 turns
- **Combat Endurance**: Maintain effort (CON roll) -> can perform an additional action
- **Quick Recovery**: Shake off an effect (CON roll) -> end a debuff prematurely

#### **Strength-based Maneuvers (STR)**
- **Destructive Charge**: Smash obstacles (STR roll) -> destroys light cover and barriers
- **Shove**: Violently push back an enemy (STR roll) -> the target is pushed back 3 meters
- **Lift**: Use the environment as a weapon (STR roll) -> improvise a heavy weapon
- **Grapple**: Immobilize an opponent (opposed STR roll) -> prevents movement

#### **Dexterity-based Maneuvers (DEX)**
- **Acrobatics**: Traverse difficult terrain (DEX roll) -> ignore terrain penalties this turn
- **Disarm**: Remove an enemy's weapon (opposed DEX roll) -> the enemy loses their weapon
- **Spectacular Dodge**: Completely avoid an attack (DEX roll) -> immunity to a targeted attack
- **Reposition**: Move without triggering opportunity attacks (DEX roll)

#### **Precision-based Maneuvers (PRE)**
- **Precision Shot**: Aim at a specific point (PRE roll) -> partially ignores armor
- **Suppressive Fire**: Force enemies to take cover (PRE roll) -> -2 to enemy actions
- **Ricochet**: Bounce a shot (PRE roll) -> hit a target behind cover
- **Disabling Shot**: Target enemy equipment (PRE roll) -> temporarily disable an item
