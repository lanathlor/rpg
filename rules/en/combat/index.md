This section presents the combat rules updated according to the defense and protection system introduced in the latest version of the handbook.
The objective is to offer a clear, fluid, and consistent framework for physical, arcanotechnological, and ranged attacks.

## Key Concepts

Combat relies on several fundamental mechanics:

- **Defense Score**: difficulty to hit a target
- **Defensive action**: Parry, Dodge or Block to counter an attack  
- **Resistances**: RMEC, RRAD and RINT absorb damage based on its physical nature
- **Flux Reserve** and **Flux Influx**: managing arcanotechnological energy in combat
- **Opportunity attack**: striking when an enemy creates an opening

A **critical failure** (natural 1) can jam a weapon or cause a technique to fail, while a **critical success** (natural 20) doubles damage.
