---
title: "Movement"
order: 3
tags: [Speed, Running, movement]
---

- A character's **speed** indicates the **number of meters traveled per turn** while running (straight line).
- Walking halves this value.
- Complex movements (jumping, climbing, crawling) may require a **DEX roll** or a specific skill.
- Leaving an enemy's melee zone triggers an **opportunity attack**: a free attack from that enemy (+1 to the attack roll).
- Characters with the **Sprint** skill can move twice per turn.
