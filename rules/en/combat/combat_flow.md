title: "Combat Flow"
order: 1
tags: [Turn, main action, minor action, combat flow]
---

A combat is divided into **turns** of 6 seconds, each representing a brief window of action.
Each turn, a character has:

-   **1 main action** (attack, spell, heavy maneuver, active defense)
-   **1 minor action** (movement, reload, quick interaction)

Turns proceed in **initiative order** until the end of combat.
