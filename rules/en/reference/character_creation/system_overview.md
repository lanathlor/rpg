The Point Buy evaluates a character's power by calculating the total cost of their abilities. Each element of a character has a point cost:

-   **Base stats**: Hit Points (HP), Movement Speed
-   **Stats**: Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Intelligence, Perception, Precision, Charisma
-   **Affinities**: Combat (melee/ranged), arcanotechnology schools, spell types, special weapons
-   **Flux System**: Reserve, per-turn regeneration, recovery
-   **Equipment**: Weapons, armor, consumables
-   **Skills**: Special talents and class abilities

### Recommended Budget

The GM will determine the appropriate point budget for the campaign.
