Base stats represent your character's fundamental physical capabilities: their **Hit Points (HP)** and their movement **Speed**.

### Hit Points (HP)

Hit Points determine your character's resistance to damage.

| HP     | Cost            | Description               |
| ------ | --------------- | ------------------------- |
| 30     | 0 pts (free)    | Reference baseline        |
| 31-33  | 1 pt            | Slightly more resilient   |
| 34-60  | 2-10 pts        | Normal progression        |
| 61-90  | 11-20 pts       | Very resilient            |
| 91-120 | 21-30 pts       | Extremely durable         |

**Formula: 1 point per 3 HP above 30 (rounded up)**

#### Examples

-   **HP 30**: 0 pts (free baseline)
-   **HP 45**: 5 pts ((45-30)/3 = 5)
-   **HP 60**: 10 pts ((60-30)/3 = 10)
-   **HP 90**: 20 pts ((90-30)/3 = 20)
-   **HP 100**: 24 pts ((100-30)/3 = 23.33, rounded to 24)

### Movement Speed

Speed determines how many meters your character can move in one turn.

| Speed   | Cost            | Description                      |
| ------- | --------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 3 m     | 0 pts (free)    | Base speed (normal human)        |
| 4 m     | 5 pts           | Faster than average              |
| 5 m     | 10 pts          | Fast                             |
| 6 m     | 15 pts          | Very fast                        |
| 7 m     | 20 pts          | Extremely fast                   |
| 8 m+    | 25+ pts         | Superhuman speed                 |

**Formula: 5 points per Speed point above 3**

#### Examples

-   **Speed 3**: 0 pts (free baseline)
-   **Speed 5**: 10 pts ((5-3)x5 = 10)
-   **Speed 6**: 15 pts ((6-3)x5 = 15)
-   **Speed 8**: 25 pts ((8-3)x5 = 25)

### Building Advice

**Typical base stats:**

-   **Spellcaster**: HP 60 (10 pts) + Speed 5 (10 pts) = **20 points**

    -   Prioritizes mobility to keep their distance

-   **Balanced fighter**: HP 90 (20 pts) + Speed 5 (10 pts) = **30 points**

    -   Good balance between resilience and mobility

-   **Heavy tank**: HP 120 (30 pts) + Speed 4 (5 pts) = **35 points**

    -   Maximizes survival at the expense of speed

-   **Agile assassin**: HP 60 (10 pts) + Speed 7 (20 pts) = **30 points**
    -   Prioritizes mobility and evasion

**Typical range: 15-35 points in base stats**

### Cost Rationale

-   **HP**: The cost increases linearly but remains moderate (1 pt/3 HP). Doubling your HP from 30 to 60 costs only 10 points.
-   **Speed**: High cost (5 pts/point) because tactical mobility is very powerful in combat. +1 m of movement can be decisive.
