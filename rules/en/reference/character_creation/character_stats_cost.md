title: "Character Stats Cost"
order: 4
---

Each stat starts at **8 for free**. Increasing a stat beyond that costs points according to the following progression:

| Stat Value | Cost per Level | Cumulative Cost from 8 |
| ---------- | -------------- | ---------------------- |
| 8          | --             | 0                      |
| 9-13       | 2 pts/level    | 2, 4, 6, 8, 10        |
| 14-15      | 3 pts/level    | 13, 16                 |
| 16-17      | 5 pts/level    | 21, 26                 |
| 18         | 6 pts          | 32                     |

### Examples

-   A stat at **10** costs: 2 + 2 = **4 points** (levels 9 and 10)
-   A stat at **14** costs: 2+2+2+2+2+3 = **13 points** (levels 9-14)
-   A stat at **16** costs: 2+2+2+2+2+3+3+5 = **21 points** (levels 9-16)

### Building Advice

A typical character with 7 stats:

-   **Main stat at 16**: 21 points
-   **Two secondary stats at 12-14**: 16-26 points
-   **Other stats at 8-11**: 0-12 points

**Average stats total: 45-60 points**
