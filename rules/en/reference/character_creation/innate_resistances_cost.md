Resistances represent your natural ability to withstand different types of damage, independently of your equipment.

### The Three Resistances

| Resistance | Abbreviation | Cost per Point | Description |
| ---------- | ------------ | -------------- | ----------- |
| Mechanical Resistance | RMEC | **3 points** | Resistance to impacts, projectiles, pressure, shockwaves |
| Radiative Resistance | RRAD | **4 points** | Resistance to fire, cold, electricity, light, radiation |
| Internal Resistance | RINT | **6 points** | Resistance to life drains, necrosis, corruption, internal attacks |

**Baseline:** All characters start with RMEC 0, RRAD 0, RINT 0 (free)

### Formula

```
Total cost = (RMEC x 3) + (RRAD x 4) + (RINT x 6)
```

### Examples

-   **RMEC +2, RRAD +1, RINT +1** (tank build): (2x3) + (1x4) + (1x6) = **16 points**
-   **RMEC +1, RRAD +1, RINT +1** (balanced build): (1x3) + (1x4) + (1x6) = **13 points**
-   **RMEC +0, RRAD +2, RINT +2** (anti-Flux arcanist): (0x3) + (2x4) + (2x6) = **20 points**
-   **RMEC +3, RRAD +0, RINT +0** (physical warrior): (3x3) + (0x4) + (0x6) = **9 points**

### Building Advice

Innate resistances represent your **natural** resistance (training, constitution, Flux mastery). They **stack** with resistances provided by equipment (armor, shields).

**Typical builds:**

-   **Light spellcaster**: RMEC +0, RRAD +1, RINT +2 = **14 points**
    -   Relies on equipment for RMEC, invests in RINT (rare to find)
-   **Balanced warrior**: RMEC +1, RRAD +1, RINT +1 = **13 points**
    -   Solid base, supplemented by heavy armor
-   **Specialized tank**: RMEC +3, RRAD +1, RINT +1 = **19 points**
    -   Maximizes RMEC to be near-impervious to physical weapons
-   **No resistance**: RMEC +0, RRAD +0, RINT +0 = **0 points**
    -   Depends entirely on equipment (economical approach)

**Typical range: 0-20 points in innate resistances**

### Cost Rationale

-   **RMEC** (3 pts): The cheapest because it's the most common damage type and equipment already provides a lot of RMEC
-   **RRAD** (4 pts): Intermediate cost, elemental damage is frequent but equipment provides less protection
-   **RINT** (6 pts): The most expensive because intentionally rare and hard to increase. Very few pieces of equipment and spells provide RINT, making innate resistances particularly valuable

**Important:** Innate resistances stack with equipment resistances. A character with +2 innate RMEC and armor with +3 RMEC will have **+5 total RMEC**.
