---
title: "GM Validation"
order: 11
---

Before finalizing your character:

1. **Check the budget**: Confirm the total points allowed by the GM
2. **Character concept**: Explain your vision of the character and their role in the group
3. **Narrative coherence**: Ensure that mechanical choices reflect the character's story
4. **Group balance**: Coordinate with other players to cover different roles

The GM may authorize minor adjustments to respect the character concept or group balance.
