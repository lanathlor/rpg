Skills are classified by tiers according to their power and utility:

### Skill Tiers

| Tier   | Cost   | Examples                                                                         |
| ------ | ------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **S**  | 18 pts | Warrior (double attacks)                                                         |
| **A**  | 15 pts | Sprint (movement x2), Electromagnetic Field, Arcanotechnology Armor              |
| **B**  | 12 pts | On Watch (priority strike), Veteran, Arcanotechnology Expert                     |
| **C**  | 10 pts | Tactician (+1 initiative), Eagle Eye (+1 PRE), Charismatic (+1 CHA)              |
| **D**  | 8 pts  | Ranger, Tracker, Engineer, Hacker, Guardian                                      |
| **E**  | 5 pts  | Quantum Sight, Terrain Expert, Nature Comprehension                              |

### Building Advice

A character typically has **2-4 skills**, for a total of **15-30 points**:

-   1 major skill (tier A or S)
-   1-2 support skills (tiers C-E)
-   Or several medium skills (tiers B-C)
