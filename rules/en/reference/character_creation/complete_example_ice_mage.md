Let's create an arcanotechnician specializing in the Frost school.

### Concept

An arcanotechnician specializing in area control and frost damage, with arcanotechnological defense.

### Base Stats (20 points)

-   **HP: 60** -- 10 pts ((60-30)/3 = 10)
-   **Speed: 5 m** -- 10 pts ((5-3)x5 = 10)

**Total: 20 points**

A typical arcanotechnician profile: decent survivability with good mobility for repositioning.

### Innate Resistances (14 points)

-   **RMEC: 0** -- 0 pts (relies on equipment for physical protection)
-   **RRAD: 1** -- 4 pts ((1x4) = 4)
-   **RINT: 2** -- 12 pts ((2x6) = 12)

**Total: 14 points**

Investment in RINT (very rare on equipment) and some RRAD to resist elemental spells. No RMEC because the tunic provides no protection anyway.

### Character Stats (49 points)

| Stat | Value | Cost |
| ---- | ----- | ---- | ---------------------- |
| STR  | 9     | 2    |
| DEX  | 9     | 2    |
| CON  | 10    | 4    |
| INT  | 16    | 21   | <- Main stat           |
| PER  | 12    | 8    |
| PRE  | 11    | 6    |
| CHA  | 11    | 6    |

**Total: 49 points**

### Affinities (81 points)

-   Ranged: 2 -- **8 pts** (for self-defense)
-   Melee: 2 -- **8 pts** (staff as last resort)
-   Frost School: 5 -- **15 pts** (main specialization)
-   Bioarcanotechnology School: 2 -- **10 pts** (support spells)
-   Destruction Type: 4 -- **20 pts** (primary damage)
-   Alteration Type: 2 -- **10 pts** (control and debuffs)
-   Displacement Type: 2 -- **10 pts** (tactical mobility)

**Total: 81 points**

### Flux System (60 points)

-   Reserve: 30 -- **30 pts** (large reserve for prolonged combat)
-   Per Turn: 8 -- **24 pts** (high regeneration)
-   Recovery: 3 -- **6 pts** (good recovery between combats)

**Total: 60 points**

### Equipment (4 points)

-   Arcanotechnological staff: 50 credits -- **1 pt**
-   Arcanotechnological tunic: 50 credits -- **1 pt**
-   Medkit: 100 credits -- **1 pt**
-   Flux Crystal: 200 credits -- **1 pt**

**Total: 4 points (400 credits)**

### Skills (20 points)

-   Arcanotechnology Armor (A) -- **15 pts** (arcanotechnological protection)
-   Quantum Sight (E) -- **5 pts** (spell detection)

**Total: 20 points**

### Summary

| Category                   | Points  | % of Total |
| -------------------------- | ------- | ---------- |
| Base Stats (HP/Speed)      | 20      | 8%         |
| Innate Resistances         | 14      | 6%         |
| Stats                      | 49      | 20%        |
| Affinities                 | 81      | 33%        |
| Flux                       | 60      | 24%        |
| Equipment                  | 4       | 2%         |
| Skills                     | 20      | 8%         |
| **TOTAL**                  | **248** | **100%**   |

This character falls in the **Mid-High** tier, with strong arcanotechnological capability thanks to high Flux and arcanotechnological affinities. The investment in RINT (Internal Resistance) allows them to better resist life drains and Flux corruption.
