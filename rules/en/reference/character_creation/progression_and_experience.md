---
title: "Progression and Experience"
order: 12
---

The Point Buy system is primarily used for initial creation. Character progression during a campaign will be managed by the experience system defined by the GM.

In general:

-   **Stats** rarely increase (major events, long training)
-   **Affinities** progress through use and training
-   The **Flux system** can improve with arcanotechnological study
-   **Equipment** is replaced and upgraded through exploration and trade
-   **Skills** are acquired through quests, mentors, or heroic accomplishments
