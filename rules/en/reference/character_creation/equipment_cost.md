title: "Equipment Cost"
order: 7
---

Equipment converts to points based on its credit value:

**Formula: Credits / 400, rounded up**

### Examples

| Equipment              | Price (credits) | Cost (points) |
| ---------------------- | --------------- | ------------- |
| Arcanotechnological staff | 50           | 1 (50/400)    |
| Pistol                 | 300             | 1             |
| Light sword            | 200             | 1             |
| Heavy sword            | 800             | 2             |
| Assault rifle          | 1,200           | 3             |
| Sniper rifle           | 2,200           | 6             |
| Heavy plate armor      | 1,200           | 3             |
| Combat exoskeleton     | 2,500           | 7             |
| Medkit                 | ~100            | 1             |

### Building Advice

A typical character spends **4-15 points on equipment** depending on their style:

-   **Light mage**: Staff + Tunic + Kits -- **~4 points**
-   **Medium warrior**: Sword + Plate Armor + Kits -- **~6 points**
-   **Specialized sniper**: Sniper Rifle + Exo + Equipment -- **~15 points**
