## Overview

Status effects are conditions applied to characters during combat or exploration. Each effect has a defined mechanical impact, a canonical saving throw to resist it, and specific removal conditions.

**Saving throw:** When a status specifies a save, the target rolls `1d20 + stat modifier` against the caster's attack roll or a fixed DC set by the spell. A successful save negates or reduces the effect.

---

## Conditions

Conditions are binary states — a character either has the condition or does not.

### Blinded

**Effect:** Cannot attack or defend; auto-fail perception checks.

**Save:** PER

**Removed by:** Duration expiry, healing effects.

### Paralyzed

**Effect:** Cannot act. Melee attacks against the target automatically hit.

**Save:** CON

**Removed by:** Duration expiry (1-2 turns).

### Incapacitated

**Effect:** Cannot act. Broken immediately upon taking damage.

**Save:** INT

**Removed by:** Taking damage or duration expiry.

### Immobilized

**Effect:** Cannot move. Can still perform actions that don't require movement.

**Save:** STR

**Removed by:** STR check or duration expiry.

### Knocked Down

**Effect:** Prone. Must spend an action to stand up. Melee attacks gain +2 against the target; ranged attacks suffer -2.

**Save:** CON

**Removed by:** Standing up (costs one action).

### Mind Controlled

**Effect:** The target is controlled by the caster and acts on the caster's orders.

**Save:** INT

**Removed by:** Taking damage (Level 1 only) or duration expiry.

### Hallucinating

**Effect:** The target perceives false stimuli. Must attack the nearest creature (ally or enemy).

**Save:** INT

**Removed by:** Duration expiry.

### Grappled

**Effect:** Movement blocked. Can still act but cannot leave the area.

**Save:** STR

**Removed by:** STR check against the effect's DC.

### Restrained

**Effect:** As Grappled, plus -2 to all attack rolls and attackers gain +2 against the target.

**Save:** STR (higher DC than Grappled)

**Removed by:** STR check against the effect's DC.

### Equipment Disabled

**Effect:** Exoskeleton bonuses and electronic device functions are nullified.

**Save:** None (affects equipment, not the person)

**Removed by:** Duration expiry.

### Suppressed

**Effect:** -4 to all actions. The target is pinned down by sustained fire or area pressure.

**Save:** None (positional — leave the area to end)

**Removed by:** Leaving the suppression area or duration expiry.

### Camouflaged

**Effect:** Hidden from detection. Opponents must succeed an opposed PER check to detect the target.

**Save:** None (opposed PER check to detect)

**Removed by:** Attacking, taking damage, or duration expiry.

## Scaling Effects

Scaling effects have variable intensity depending on the spell level or stacking.

### Frostbite

**Effect:** -1 speed and -1 hit score per stack. At 5 stacks, triggers an explosion dealing 2d20+8 damage and removes 5 stacks.

**Save:** CON

**Duration:** 2 turns per stack.

**Stacks:** Yes (up to 5 before detonation).

### Burning

**Effect:** Takes fire damage at the start of each turn. Damage varies by spell level (1d4 to 4d6 per turn).

**Save:** DEX

**Duration:** 2-4 turns depending on spell level.

**Stacks:** No (higher level replaces lower).

### Weakened

**Effect:** -X to all rolls (X varies by spell level, typically -1 to -4).

**Save:** CON

**Duration:** 2-3 turns.

### Slowed

**Effect:** -X to movement speed (X varies by spell level).

**Save:** CON

**Duration:** Varies by spell.

### Fatigued

**Effect:** -X to all rolls and movement speed halved. Self-inflicted backlash from techniques like Combat Overdrive.

**Save:** None (self-inflicted, always applies)

**Duration:** 2-3 turns.

## Buffs

Buffs are beneficial effects applied to self or allies.

### Hasted

**Effect:** Increased movement speed and additional actions per turn. Exact values depend on the spell.

**Duration:** Varies by spell.

### Shielded

**Effect:** +X to Defense Score and/or damage reduction.

**Duration:** Varies by spell.

### Reinforced

**Effect:** +X to all resistances (RMEC, RRAD, RINT).

**Duration:** Varies by spell.

### Deflecting

**Effect:** Automatically deflects incoming projectiles.

**Duration:** Varies by spell.

### Arcanotechnically Resistant

**Effect:** +X to resistance against arcanotechnic effects specifically.

**Duration:** Varies by spell.
