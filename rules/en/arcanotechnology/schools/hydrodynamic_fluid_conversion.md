**Principle:** Transformation of Flux into energy controlling water and its states.

**Strengths:**

- Terrain control.
- Versatile with frost and fire.
- Can support allies (cooling, purification).

**Weaknesses:**

- Requires a water source.
- Slow in dry environments.
