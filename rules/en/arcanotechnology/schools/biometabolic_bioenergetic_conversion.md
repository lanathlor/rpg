---
title: "Biometabolic -- Bioenergetic Conversion"
order: 7
---

**Principle:** Manipulates the vital field of organisms: extraction, transfer, or inversion of biological energy.

**Strengths:**

- Life drain, energy theft.
- Summoning of servants.
- Self-sustenance through vital absorption.

**Weaknesses:**

- Mental instability of the user.
- Progressive corruption.
- Countered by light arcanotechnology.
