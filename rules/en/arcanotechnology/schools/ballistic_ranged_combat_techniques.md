title: "Ballistic -- Ranged Combat Techniques"
order: 19
---

**Principle:** Shooting and ranged combat techniques developed through specialized training with ranged weapons. Mastery of precision, rate of fire, and specialized ammunition.

**Strengths:**

- No Flux consumption.
- Effective at long range.
- Enhances ranged armament capabilities.
- Enables the use of specialized ammunition.

**Weaknesses:**

- Dependent on ammunition and equipment.
- Requires specialized shooting training.
- Vulnerable in close combat.
