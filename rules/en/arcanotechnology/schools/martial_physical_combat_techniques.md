---
title: 'Martial -- Physical Combat Techniques'
order: 18
---

**Principle:** Close combat techniques developed through training and experience, involving no arcanotechnological manipulation. Based on mastery of the body, melee weapons, and strategy.

**Strengths:**

-   No Flux consumption.
-   Functions in all circumstances.
-   Enhances melee armament capabilities.

**Weaknesses:**

-   Limited by physical capabilities.
-   Require long and rigorous training.
-   Ineffective against certain arcanotechnological protections.
