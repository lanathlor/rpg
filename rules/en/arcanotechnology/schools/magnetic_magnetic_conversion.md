**Principle:** Generation of powerful magnetic fields via Flux.

**Strengths:**

- Control of metal and shielding.
- Technology neutralization.
- Magnetic levitation.

**Weaknesses:**

- Ineffective on non-ferrous materials.
- Field instability at high intensity.
