**Principle:** Transformation of Flux into an ordered electromagnetic field (lightning, discharges).

**Strengths:**

- Near-instantaneous activation speed.
- Chain damage between targets.
- Synergy with technology.

**Weaknesses:**

- Risk of bouncing to allies.
- Sensitive to grounding.
- High consumption in humid environments.
