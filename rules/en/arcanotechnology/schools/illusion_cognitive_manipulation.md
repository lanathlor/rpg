**Principle:** Transformation of Flux into informational signals that alter cognitive perception, sensory data, and information flows.

**Strengths:**

- Manipulation of senses and cognition (confusion, hallucinations, fear).
- Data extraction and insertion.
- Low Flux cost.

**Weaknesses:**

- Ineffective against shielded minds and hardened systems.
- Dispelled by arcanotechnic clarity effects.
- No direct physical effect.
