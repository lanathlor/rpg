**Principle:** Conversion of Flux into coherent photonic flow contained within a photoarcanotechnological field.
Allows the creation of **solid light walls** via confinement and energetic feedback.

**Strengths:**

-   Stable and durable barriers (light wall).
-   Partial self-stabilization: low maintenance consumption.
-   Capable of reflecting or dispersing energy and projectiles.

**Weaknesses:**

-   High initial consumption.
-   Vulnerable to arcanotechnological interference (shadow, pure).
-   Dephases under energetic overload.
