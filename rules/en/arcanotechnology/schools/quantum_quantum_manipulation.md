title: "Quantum -- Quantum Manipulation"
order: 8
---

**Principle:** Alteration of subatomic bonds and states of matter (spin, charge, phase).

**Strengths:**

- Teleportation, transmutation, density alteration.
- Universal interaction with all other arcanotechnology.
- Cancels physical effects without destruction.

**Weaknesses:**

- Highly unstable.
- Difficult to master.
- Variable and unpredictable consumption.
