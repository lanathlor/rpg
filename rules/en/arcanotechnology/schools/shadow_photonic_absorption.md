title: "Shadow -- Photonic Absorption"
order: 6
tags: [Shadow]
---

**Principle:** Reconverts incident photons into dissipated Flux, creating zones of total visible light absorption. The process is irreversible — photonic energy dissipates into the quantum vacuum, in accordance with Primic's Law.

**Strengths:**

- Total optical stealth (camouflage, invisibility).
- Disrupts optical sensors and visual perception.
- Area concealment.

**Weaknesses:**

- No effect on non-optical sensors (thermal, sonar, radar).
- Directly countered by the Light school.
- Ineffective in total darkness (no photons to absorb).
