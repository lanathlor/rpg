---
title: 'Chronodynamic -- Temporal Conversion'
order: 15
---

**Principle:** Manipulation of local time flow through compression or dilation of Flux.

**Strengths:**

-   Alteration of perceived time.
-   Stabilization or desynchronization of Flux flows.

**Weaknesses:**

-   Risk of temporal distortion.
-   Cumulative counter-effect (temporal rebound).
