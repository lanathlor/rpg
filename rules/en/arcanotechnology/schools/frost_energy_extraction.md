---
title: 'Frost -- Energy Extraction'
order: 2
tags: [Frost, Frost School]
---

**Principle:** Removal of ambient kinetic energy, creating a negative thermal gradient.

**Strengths:**

-   Zone control (slowing, immobilization).
-   Excellent energy efficiency.
-   Can reinforce defense through local cooling.

**Weaknesses:**

-   Low damage.
-   Slow to act.
-   Fragile in hot environments.
