---
title: 'Aerokinetic -- Atmospheric Conversion'
order: 10
tags: [Aerokinetic]
---

**Principle:** Conversion of Flux into macroscopic mechanical energy (wind, pressure).

**Strengths:**

-   Zone control.
-   Projectile deflection.
-   Disrupts enemy fire.

**Weaknesses:**

-   Ineffective in vacuum.
-   Low precision.
