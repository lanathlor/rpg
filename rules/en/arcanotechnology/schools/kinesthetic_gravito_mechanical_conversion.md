---
title: "Kinesthetic -- Gravito-Mechanical Conversion"
order: 4
---

**Principle:** Transformation of Flux into mechanical work (thrust, pressure, local gravity).

**Strengths:**

- Terrain control.
- Affects all matter.
- Versatile between attack and defense.

**Weaknesses:**

- Very costly depending on target mass.
- Risk of matter deformation.
- Slow reaction in unstable environments.
