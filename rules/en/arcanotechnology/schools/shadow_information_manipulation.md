---
title: "Shadow -- Information Manipulation"
order: 6
tags: [Shadow]
---

**Principle:** Modifies the propagation of light and sound waves, acting on perception rather than matter.

**Strengths:**

- Total stealth.
- Light mental control (confusion, fear).
- Cancels or diverts optical/acoustic detection.

**Weaknesses:**

- No real physical effect.
- Ineffective against AI and machines.
- Instantly dispelled by pure light.
